FAQ

Are mining returns guaranteed?

No. MineCore does not promise guaranteed returns.

Is a submitted TXID automatically approved?

No. The backend requires payment verification before approval.

Are displayed mining numbers simulated?

No. When real mining infrastructure is unavailable, the mining page explicitly says so.

Can a completed withdrawal have a fabricated TXID?

No. Completion requires a TXID returned by the configured payout provider.
